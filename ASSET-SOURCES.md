## Shop image crop review

- shop-1.jpg is portrait. It supports the card silhouette through a tight crop of black chairs and warm timber. Center crop works; at wide banner sizes it loses the sense of a full room. Use around 50% x / 52% y.
- shop-2.jpg is wide, with a centered clock, framed photos and red leather chairs. Strong vintage silhouette. Best fit for The Old School or Sololaki Grooming.
- shop-3.jpg is wide, with a repeated rhythm of cognac chairs and brick. Strong warm geometric silhouette. Good fit for Blade & Bourbon or Northside Barber.
- shop-4.jpg is wide, with black furniture, geometric ceiling light and white panels. Strong modern silhouette. Best fit for Forma Studio; the current rotating data gives Forma the vintage shop-2 image instead.
- All four hold up in landscape shop cards and small gallery tiles. Keep equal image heights with object-fit: cover. Shop-1 needs the most cropping; the other three naturally fit.
- The current shop gallery pairs different shops' interiors as if they were one location. For visual coherence, use the primary shop image plus haircut/process details, or show one interior photo, rather than unrelated black-chair, red-chair and marble interiors in the same location gallery.
